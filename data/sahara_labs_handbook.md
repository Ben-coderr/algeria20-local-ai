# Sahara Labs — Employee & Intern Handbook (fictional)

## About us
Sahara Labs is a fictional AI startup founded in 2023 with offices in Oran and Algiers.
We build Arabic and French language tools for local businesses.

## Working hours
Core hours are 9:30 to 16:00, Sunday to Thursday.
Every Thursday is a remote work day for all employees.

## Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year.
Interns receive 2 days of leave per month of internship.
Leave requests must be sent at least 10 days in advance to your team lead.

## Internships
Internships last 6 months.
Interns receive a monthly allowance of 40,000 DZD.
Each intern is assigned a mentor during the first week.
At the end of the internship, interns present a demo to the whole company.

## Equipment
Every new employee receives a laptop budget of 180,000 DZD.
Employees may keep their laptop after 3 years of service.

## Learning
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 60,000 DZD for courses, books or conferences.
Attending Algeria 2.0 is fully covered by the company.
